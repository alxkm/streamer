# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about code, not about people.

## What is expected

- Keep feedback about the change, not its author.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline an operator that does not fit the scope described in
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`. That is a scope decision, not a judgement of the contributor.
- English in issues, pull requests, commits and comments, so everyone can follow along.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, and deliberate derailing of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or contacting the maintainer directly
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide or
delete comments, and block accounts that do not follow this document.
